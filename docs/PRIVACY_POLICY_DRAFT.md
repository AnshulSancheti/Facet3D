Facet Escape3D stores puzzle progress, preferences, hint balance, daily challenge results and local play statistics on your device. The game requires no account. Local diagnostic/event records do not have a remote analytics service configured. You can remove local game data through Android app storage settings or uninstalling; this resets progress.

The Android app includes Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs through the Poing Studios AdMob plugin. Advertising is currently disabled in application configuration. If a distributed version enables advertising, Google may process device/IP information, approximate location, identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, depending on settings and consent. Review [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the final version-specific SDK disclosures. Inclusion of an SDK must be reviewed for automatic behavior before making any no-collection claim.

When required, the advertising privacy service presents jurisdiction-aware choices. Where available, Settings provides Advertising Privacy Options to review choices. Android provides controls for advertising identifiers. Puzzles remain playable without ads or internet access; rewarded hint advertisements are optional.

Advertising service data retention, security and deletion are governed by Google's actual service policies; no precise retention period is asserted in this draft.
